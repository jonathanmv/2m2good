The part we forget

A long stretch at the computer can feel like a long stretch in one shape.

Not a diagnosis. Not a performance test. Just a useful moment to change position and let your neck, shoulders, hands + wrists, or lower back have a turn.

  • Turn a little. Stay comfortably within your range.

  • Let them move a little, without making a project of it.

  • Open and soften your hands. A small change is enough.

  • Shift gently. A different position can be the whole point.

02 / keep it easy

A pause with a beginning and an end.

2m2better notices a long stretch of active keyboard or mouse use, then offers one clear check-in. No maze of settings. No disappearing decision.

  1. 01

    Notice

    A small check-in arrives after you have been at the computer for a while.

  2. 02

    Choose

    Start, Later, and Tomorrow stay visible. There is no wrong answer.

  3. 03

    Move

    Follow one gentle, visible two-minute reset. Spoken guidance is optional.

  4. 04

    Return

    Finish, stop, or skip whenever you need. Then carry on with your day.

03 / small on purpose

Helpful, not hungry for your attention.

There is no account to make, feed to maintain, or wellness score to chase. The companion should give a little room back, not ask for more of it.

  • 01

    Local by default

    The break experience processes locally on your Mac. It stores only the small amount of preference and movement-history state needed for the companion.

  • 02

    Coarse activity, never content

    It reads aggregate time since keyboard or pointer activity. It does not read key values, pointer coordinates, app content, or which app you are using.

  • 03

    No agent connection

    2m2better does not detect, watch, or coordinate coding agents. An in-between moment is simply a nice time to choose a pause.

  • 04

    Updates are explicit

    The optional updater checks GitHub Releases only when you ask it to. The developer preview is ad-hoc signed and not notarized.
