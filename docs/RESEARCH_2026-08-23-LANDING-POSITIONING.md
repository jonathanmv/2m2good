# Landing-page positioning research — 2026-08-23

**Method:** checked the public landing pages below on 2026-08-23 UTC. The existing body-area and positioning scout reports informed the product boundary, but this note records a fresh competitor pass. Claims below are observations of page copy and section structure, not endorsements or efficacy findings.

## Current competitor observations

- [LookAway](https://lookaway.com/) — Hero: “The Mac app your body thanks you for” and “Breaks that don't break your flow.” Follow-on sections cover heads-up/snooze reminders, workflow-aware pausing, posture and blink reminders, iPhone/iPad continuity, and customization before a Mac download CTA. It positions quiet screen-habit support around uninterrupted flow.
- [DeskRest](https://deskrest.com/) — Hero: “Smart Break Reminders for Healthy Eyes, Posture & Productivity.” The page moves through short and long break examples, meeting/video/focus detection, streak visualizations, reminder styles, testimonials, custom exercises, posture flashes, and a download CTA. It bundles body support with habit and productivity language.
- [Moova](https://getmoova.app/) — Hero: “Stop Sitting All Day with 3-Minute Movement Breaks.” Sections follow with quick activity breaks, 200+ expert-designed targeted exercises, scheduled reminders/personalized plans, social proof, and an app download CTA. It sells a broad movement library for busy people rather than a desktop moment.
- [Stretchly](https://hovancik.net/stretchly/) — The compact public page leads with “The break time reminder app,” describes a cross-platform open-source app, and routes quickly to Downloads/About/Research/Sponsor. Its claim is simple, configurable break reminding rather than guided body support.
- [BreakTimer](https://breaktimer.app/) — Hero: “Save yourself from RSI and eye strain,” followed by configurable schedules, custom colors/messages, working hours, smart notifications, platform downloads, and a source link. It owns the configurable reminder utility category, with health language 2m2better will not adopt.
- [Opal](https://www.opal.so/) — Focus-product hero: “Attention on autopilot.” The page then presents focus rules, a focus timer, a score, milestone gems, testimonials, cross-platform availability, and measured focus outcomes. It uses measurable attention control and reward mechanics, not a body-support break.

## Pattern to borrow, without copying

The strongest shared pattern is **one clear promise → a short sequence of concrete product moments → a visible CTA**. The most relevant pages also show the product in context instead of leading with a settings list. 2m2better will use that structure as: a calm area-specific promise, a concise check-in, a scroll-led playable preview, local/privacy proof, and a transparent developer-preview installer.

## Deliberate non-copying choices

2m2better will **not** copy:

- posture correction, pain/RSI/eye-strain treatment or prevention, medical outcomes, or productivity-pressure claims;
- “breaks that don't break your flow” as a headline or testimonials/quantified outcome proof;
- a dashboard, streaks, scores, milestone rewards, personalized plans, broad exercise catalog, or feature-heavy settings tour;
- a short-break/long-break schedule comparison or a page led by exact timing, standing-only language, a six-movement breakdown, or 20-second mechanics;
- a permission-heavy demo, account, analytics, payments, paywall, or signed-consumer-download implication.

The resulting wedge is a small, permission-based movement moment: **2 mins to better your [area]**, rotating accessibly through neck, shoulders, hands + wrists, and lower back. The site demonstrates the interaction with buttons, makes the orb's break-proximity color a supporting cue, and labels the installer as an auditable developer preview rather than a finished consumer release.
