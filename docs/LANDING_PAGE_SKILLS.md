## How this fits 2m2better

[`elayadesign/ai-design-skills`'s `landing-page-design`](https://github.com/elayadesign/ai-design-skills/blob/main/skills/landing-page-design/SKILL.md) is the primary end-to-end workflow: use its intake, one-offer/one-audience/one-action framing, page structure, benefit-led copy, proof, objection handling, SEO, and visual review sequence as the planning spine. It complements rather than overrides the project sources of truth: [`docs/BRAND_DIRECTION.md`](BRAND_DIRECTION.md) owns 2m2better's promise, voice, lowercase name, product character, and guardrails; [`docs/DESIGN_TOKENS.md`](DESIGN_TOKENS.md) owns the tide-and-clay visual contract and semantic token usage; and [`marketing/README.md`](../marketing/README.md) owns the local implementation and validation commands. The external workflow's font, spacing, color, motion, CTA, and section defaults are suggestions to evaluate against those project documents, not replacements for them.

## Recommended sequence for 2m2better

1. **Strategy and intake:** establish audience, promise, one primary action, objections, proof, and success criteria with [`landing-page-design`](https://github.com/elayadesign/ai-design-skills/blob/main/skills/landing-page-design/SKILL.md), [`design-brief`](https://github.com/Owl-Listener/designer-skills/blob/main/ux-strategy/skills/design-brief/SKILL.md), [`jobs-to-be-done`](https://github.com/Owl-Listener/designer-skills/blob/main/design-research/skills/jobs-to-be-done/SKILL.md), and [`competitive-analysis`](https://github.com/Owl-Listener/designer-skills/blob/main/ux-strategy/skills/competitive-analysis/SKILL.md), while checking 2m2better's brand guardrails.
2. **Structure and copy:** choose the page argument, hierarchy, hero, proof, how-it-works flow, FAQs, and CTA with [`landing-page`](https://github.com/MengTo/Skills/blob/main/agent-skills/web-design/landing-page/SKILL.md), [`narrative-arc`](https://github.com/codeswithroh/tastemaker/blob/main/skills/tastemaker/references/narrative-arc.md), [`macrostructures`](https://github.com/codeswithroh/tastemaker/blob/main/skills/tastemaker/references/macrostructures.md), [`better-writing`](https://github.com/jakubkrehel/skills/blob/main/skills/better-writing/SKILL.md), and [`ux-writing`](https://github.com/Owl-Listener/designer-skills/blob/main/designer-toolkit/skills/ux-writing/SKILL.md).
3. **Visual system and assets:** translate the approved direction into existing tokens, responsive layout, typography, and honest product imagery using [`better-layout`](https://github.com/jakubkrehel/skills/blob/main/skills/better-layout/SKILL.md), [`better-typography`](https://github.com/jakubkrehel/skills/blob/main/skills/better-typography/SKILL.md), [`better-colors`](https://github.com/jakubkrehel/skills/blob/main/skills/better-colors/SKILL.md), [`asset-curation`](https://github.com/codeswithroh/tastemaker/blob/main/skills/tastemaker/references/asset-curation.md), and [`gpt-image-2`](https://github.com/ConardLi/garden-skills/tree/main/skills/gpt-image-2), without bypassing the project tokens.
4. **Motion:** add only purposeful, restrained, token-backed motion with [`animation-systems`](https://github.com/MengTo/Skills/blob/main/agent-skills/web-design/animation-systems/SKILL.md), [`animate`](https://github.com/emilkowalski/skills/blob/main/skills/animate/SKILL.md), [`animation-principles`](https://github.com/Owl-Listener/designer-skills/blob/main/interaction-design/skills/animation-principles/SKILL.md), and [`motion-system`](https://github.com/Owl-Listener/designer-skills/blob/main/design-systems/skills/motion-system/SKILL.md).
5. **Implementation:** build section by section in the existing marketing stack, keeping product proof honest and using [`web-design-engineer`](https://github.com/ConardLi/garden-skills/blob/main/skills/web-design-engineer/SKILL.md), [`product-proof-saas`](https://github.com/MengTo/Skills/blob/main/agent-skills/web-design/product-proof-saas/SKILL.md), [`component-sourcing`](https://github.com/codeswithroh/tastemaker/blob/main/skills/tastemaker/references/component-sourcing.md), and [`handoff-spec`](https://github.com/Owl-Listener/designer-skills/blob/main/design-ops/skills/handoff-spec/SKILL.md) as needed.
6. **Accessibility and critique:** run keyboard, screen-reader, reduced-motion, contrast, responsive, hierarchy, and composition passes with [`better-accessibility`](https://github.com/jakubkrehel/skills/blob/main/skills/better-accessibility/SKILL.md), [`accessibility-audit`](https://github.com/Owl-Listener/designer-skills/blob/main/design-systems/skills/accessibility-audit/SKILL.md), [`heuristic-evaluation`](https://github.com/Owl-Listener/designer-skills/blob/main/prototyping-testing/skills/heuristic-evaluation/SKILL.md), and the [`visual-critique`](https://github.com/Owl-Listener/designer-skills/tree/main/visual-critique) set.
7. **Final QA and delivery:** verify rendered output, token adoption, motion performance, full-page evidence, handoff, and the project's own checks with [`design-qa-checklist`](https://github.com/Owl-Listener/designer-skills/blob/main/design-ops/skills/design-qa-checklist/SKILL.md), [`design-token-audit`](https://github.com/Owl-Listener/designer-skills/blob/main/designer-toolkit/skills/design-token-audit/SKILL.md), [`iterate-until-verified`](https://github.com/MengTo/Skills/blob/main/agent-skills/codex/iterate-until-verified/SKILL.md), and [`optimize-web-animations`](https://github.com/MengTo/Skills/blob/main/agent-skills/codex/optimize-web-animations/SKILL.md), then follow [`marketing/README.md`](../marketing/README.md).
